\subsection*{Measurement results}

\noindent
The boost converter was tested with a 5\,V input from the energy store and an external PWM gate drive switching at 10\,kHz. Figure~\ref{fig:boost_drain_output} shows the MOSFET drain voltage (the switching node) together with the converter output voltage at a duty cycle of 40\,\%. The output settles at 9.77\,V, close to the 10\,V target for the V2 rail, with a peak-to-peak ripple of only about 142\,mV --- roughly 1.5\,\% of the output voltage. The drain waveform confirms clean, well-defined switching at 10\,kHz, and since the output level is set by the PWM duty cycle, the controller can regulate V2 to the desired value.
