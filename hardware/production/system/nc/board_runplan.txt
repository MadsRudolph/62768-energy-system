DOUBLE-SIDED run plan: board  [Roland SRM-20 (G-code)]  registration: FIDUCIAL

FIDUCIAL mode: 4 reference holes inside the board corners, drilled 2.00 mm (through the 1.7 mm stock only - NOT into the bed). Onboard holes stay in the finished board; pick corners clear of copper.

0. Set XY zero ONCE and do NOT re-zero XY for the bottom side.
1. board_align: drills the 4 fiducial holes. Bottom side: board_bottom_drill.nc with one 0.8 mm bit (plunge holes that fit, interpolate larger ones). Then board_bottom_traces.
2. FLIP the board left-to-right and re-place it (no pins needed).
   Re-zero Z on the new surface.
3. Probe each fiducial and record its measured X/Y. Nominal (perfect-
   flip) positions to probe near:
    fiducial 1: X209.100 Y5.500
    fiducial 2: X15.000 Y5.500
    fiducial 3: X15.000 Y147.500
    fiducial 4: X209.100 Y147.500
4. In the app, enter/capture the measured X/Y and 'Fit & export top
   traces' (fit: rotation + translation). Check the RMS - a high value means a bad
   re-placement; re-seat and re-probe before cutting.
5. board_top_traces (now warped to the fit): cut it.
6. board_cutout LAST.
Estimated run time (excludes tool changes, spin-up and pauses):
   board_align.nc: ~52s
   board_bottom_drill.nc: ~37m 26s
   board_bottom_traces.nc: ~1h 09m
   board_top_traces.nc: ~1h 06m
   board_cutout.nc: ~11m 16s
   TOTAL: ~3h 05m
