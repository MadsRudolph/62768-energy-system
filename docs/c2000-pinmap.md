# C2000 LAUNCHXL-F28027 — pin map & 3.3 V sensing plan

The contract between firmware and hardware **for the TI port**. The C2000 runs the
**V1 motor PID + system monitoring**. (MPPT lives on the Arduino Nano with the INA219 over
I²C — `V_PV`/`I_PV` are **not** on the C2000.)

> This is the C2000 (3.3 V) replacement for [`mega-pinmap.md`](mega-pinmap.md), which is
> sized for a 5 V Arduino. **Every divider and op-amp gain here is scaled to 0–3.3 V** —
> the C2000 ADC absolute-max is **3.3 V** and overvoltage kills the chip.

Header pins are from the LaunchPad User's Guide **Table 1** (`docs/datasheets/
Texas_Instruments-LAUNCHXL-F28027-datasheet.pdf`, p.8). ADC full scale = **3.3 V, 12-bit**
→ `count = V_pin · 4095 / 3.3` (verified: 1.65 V → ~2048, `C2000_HW_TEST_RESULTS.md`).

---

## Which pins are actually broken out (don't guess — A5 is NOT on a header)

| Type | Available on headers |
|---|---|
| **ADC inputs** | ADCINA0, A1, A2, A3, A4, A6, A7, ADCINB1, B2, B3, B4, B6, B7 |
| **PWM outputs** | EPWM1A/1B, EPWM2A/2B, EPWM3A/3B, EPWM4A/4B |
| **GND** | **J2-1**, **J5-2** |
| **3.3 V / 5 V rails** | J1-1 (+3.3 V), J5-1 (+5 V) |

Not broken out: ADCINA5, ADCINB0/B5. (The `MotorControl2026` lecture model used ADCINA5 —
that's why it had to be remapped to A1.)

## Assignments (✓ = physically verified in the bring-up tests)

| Signal | ADC channel | Header pin | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Motor PWM out** | EPWM1A | **J6-1** ✓ | drive circuit (→ CNY17 → IR2110 → IRF540N) |
| **V1** rectifier bus | ADCINA1 | **J5-5** ✓ | the PID feedback |
| **V2** load | ADCINA0 | **J5-6** ✓ | monitoring / boost loop check |
| **V3** store | ADCINA3 | J5-4 | monitoring |
| **I1** generator current | ADCINA4 | **J1-6** ✓ | monitoring (Krav 2) |
| **I2** load current | ADCINA6 | J1-2 | monitoring |
| **I3** store current (**bidirectional**) | ADCINA7 | J5-3 | monitoring |
| **GND** | — | **J2-1** | common with drive circuit + power ground |

> Board switches for deploy/monitor (from the bring-up log): **S1 all-UP** (TRSTn up, or
> JTAG fails `-1015`), **S4 UP** (routes SCI to the XDS100 for Monitor & Tune over COM16).
> Flash standalone with the device-specific **`f28027.ccxml`**.

## Voltage dividers (build these — 1 % resistors, 3.3 V scaling)

`count → volts` in firmware: `V = count · (3.3/4095) · (R_high + R_low)/R_low`

| Rail | Nominal | Design max | R_high / R_low | Ratio | Nominal → ADC | Clamp at | Scale (V/count) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **V1** | 15 V | ~33 V (open-loop spike) | **10k / 1k** | ÷11 | 15 V → **1.36 V** | 36.3 V → 3.3 V | 0.008865 |
| **V2** | ~10 V (TBC) | ~21 V | **12k / 2k** | ÷7 | 10 V → **1.43 V** | 23.1 V → 3.3 V | 0.005641 |
| **V3** | 5 V | ~10 V | **2k / 1k** | ÷3 | 5 V → **1.65 V** | 9.9 V → 3.3 V | 0.002418 |

- **Low source impedance on purpose** (V1: 10k‖1k ≈ 0.9 kΩ) so the C2000 S/H settles in a
  short acquisition window — a 100k/10k divider would need an op-amp buffer.
- **Protect every ADC pin:** **series 1 kΩ + a 3.0 V zener to GND + 10 nF to GND** at the
  pin. The 3.0 V zener clamps below the 3.3 V abs-max with margin.
- **Calibrate** each scale constant against a multimeter once wired (tolerance + zener leak).

## Current sensing (discrete: 1 Ω shunt + MCP601, Krav 10) — 3.3 V scaling

Low-side shunt, non-inverting op-amp, gain sized so **design-max current ≈ 3.0 V** (was 4.5 V
on the 5 V Arduino):

| Channel | I max | Shunt | Amp gain | Mid-bias | V/A at pin | Firmware |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **I1** generator | 400 mA | 1 Ω | **7.5×** | — | 7.5 | `I1 = count·(3.3/4095)/7.5` |
| **I2** load | 300 mA | 1 Ω | **10×** | — | 10 | `I2 = count·(3.3/4095)/10` |
| **I3** store (bidir) | ±300 mA | 1 Ω | **5×** | **1.65 V** | 5 | `I3 = (count·(3.3/4095) − 1.65)/5` |

> **I3 mid-scale = 1.65 V, not 2.5 V.** The store charges *and* discharges, so the amp must
> idle at half of the 3.3 V scale. Bias it with a **10k/10k divider off the +3.3 V rail**
> into the non-inverting summing node. (The old 5 V plan used 2.5 V — wrong for the C2000.)

Shunt dissipation is tiny (I1: 0.4²·1 = 0.16 W → use a ≥0.25 W shunt).

## Monitoring stream (SCI / USB-UART, 1 Hz — Krav 13/14, optional)

CSV line, newline-terminated, 3 decimals (the C2000 only carries the rails it senses; the PV
numbers come from the Arduino MPPT node):

```
V1=14.98,V2=10.02,V3=5.01,I1=0.142,I2=0.098,I3=-0.021
```

Emit at a 1 s subrate (Rate Transition from the control rate). Note Monitor & Tune
(External Mode) **owns the same SCI/COM16** — you can't run both a tuning session and a free
serial monitor at once.

## The board that implements this

A KiCad laser PCB carries all of the above — the **C2000 feedback/sensor board**,
`hardware/kicad/boards/c2000_feedback/`. Generated by
`hardware/kicad/tools/generators/build_c2000_feedback.py`; production DXF + Gerbers in
`hardware/kicad/production/c2000_feedback/`. ERC 0, DRC 0, 0 unconnected, 0 vias,
12 wire bridges (deterministic signal-flow placement in the `PLACE` dict). Layout:
power/return **screw terminals on the left edge**, **short
headers to the LaunchPad on the right** —

| Header | Pins → LaunchPad |
|---|---|
| **J2** (1×2) | +3V3 (J1-1), GND (J2-1) |
| **J4** (1×3) | ADCV1→J5-5, ADCV2→J5-6, ADCV3→J5-4 |
| **J6** (1×3) | ISENSE1→J1-6, ISENSE2→J1-2, ISENSE3→J5-3 |
| **J8** (1×2) | PWMMCU← EPWM1A (J6-1), GND |
| **J10** | PWM → drive-circuit opto (after the 220 Ω) |

See `PCB_RESULTS.md` for the full board note and the pre-etch checklist.

## What's NOT here (lives on the Arduino Nano)

`V_PV`, `I_PV`, and the MPPT P&O loop — measured by the **INA219** over I²C and driven by an
Arduino PWM into the PV-side buck. Independent MCU, independent ground domain. See
`firmware/MPPT/`.
